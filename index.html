My story

Scroll to follow the path from 2006 to today.

2006

Where it began

I was born in Beni Mazar, El Minya, Egypt.

Around age 10

The family computer

My father brought a computer home: a Core 2 Duo, 2 GB of DDR3 RAM and a 500 GB hard drive. It was modest even then, but to a child it was a whole world to explore.

The questions

Curiosity before code

Before I wrote a single line, I wanted to know how it all works. How does the machine know I pressed the letter Alef? Why does one game run on my PC and another doesn’t? I used to take my games apart to see inside them. Relatives studying computing and engineering gave me real answers, and curiosity turned into direction. Computers stopped being toys and became the thing I wanted to understand from the inside.

Around 2019

A first channel, and a lesson

In 2nd prep I started a YouTube channel and posted for three months. Then I stopped: I had no goal, no vision and no strong material. It taught me early that content needs a purpose before it needs an audience.

Around 2020

Hello World, I’m Sameh!

In 3rd prep I wrote my first code, an HTML heading that said “Hello World, I’m Sameh!”. One line, but it was the first time the screen said something I decided.

Around 2022

My first real project

In 2nd secondary I built a to-do web app with drag and drop between boards such as done, not yet, urgent and not urgent. It was my first time taking an idea from nothing to something that worked.

Around 2023

Drawn to systems

I kept being pulled toward servers, networks and how systems fit together, without knowing the field had a name.

2024

University, on purpose

I chose Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence at Minia National University because I wanted my passion and my academic path to point the same way, and to learn enough to build things, not only to pass exams.

The turning point

How much I hadn’t touched

After years of study, I sat with someone who spoke about the field in real depth, and I saw how much I hadn’t reached yet. It was uncomfortable, and it was the most useful moment of my learning. I decided to become truly strong in my field, and my experience, my path, my understanding and my study have moved up a level since.

Around 2025

Going deep, and teaching properly

I finally learned the name of what I’d been drawn to: DevOps and infrastructure, and I went in deep. I also started Tech House – CS, an Arabic channel that teaches computer science from the beginning, this time with a clear purpose.

2025 – today

Building and shipping

I like to build something from its first step until I see it running, and I like the problems along the way. I started with small tools, then moved to bigger, documented projects: a routed Cisco network, a multi-router WAN, an Active Directory lab, and the first stable release of the Infrastructure Health Checker (v1.0.0).

A hard lesson

The home lab that broke

My Ubuntu home lab stopped working, and bringing it back took about a month and a half. The package repositories it updates from were failing, and the network interfaces had problems too. I traced each issue until the server ran again. I learned a lot from it, and this kind of troubleshooting is exactly what I now write down in my lab documentation.

Now

Linux automation

I’m building a Bash toolkit for Linux server operations, and I document every project on GitHub as I go.

2028

The goal

Graduate and work as a DevOps / infrastructure engineer who builds, documents and maintains real systems.
